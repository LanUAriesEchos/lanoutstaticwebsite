About AllyCraft

AllyCraft is a bespoke Minecraft modpack, uniquely designed for the ASUS ROG Ally and Steam Deck. It brings a finely tuned version of Minecraft to handheld gaming enthusiasts. The pack runs the game much more smoothly and offers seamless controller support, making it the perfect option for portable gaming on high-performance handhelds. It can also be used on desktops!

Now with shader support!

Experience Minecraft with shaders on your ASUS ROG Ally and Valve Steam Deck!

Please set your device to maximum power mode for best performance with shaders, unless you are using a very light shader.

We recommend Complementary Reimagined on its medium setting in the shader options menu.

First launch setup

  1. Set to game pad mode.
  2. Download the library (it will prompt you).
  3. Relaunch!

Two versions

The pack comes in two versions: Midnight Controls and Controlify. Download RC for Controlify, or RMC for Midnight.
