Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

We do not share ANY of your data.

Not with advertisers, not with data brokers, not with partners, not with anyone. We don't sell it, rent it, trade it or give it away.

1. Who we are

TheLanout ("we", "us") runs this website and the tools and projects listed on it. This policy explains what happens to information when you visit thelanout.net and use the pages that make up this site.

2. Data we never share

We do not share, sell, rent, trade or disclose any of your data to any third party, for any reason, including for advertising, marketing, profiling or analytics. This applies to everything described in this policy, with one narrow exception: we may be compelled to act where the law strictly requires it (see section 8).

3. What we collect

  • No accounts. You don't need to sign up, and we don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal details to browse the site.
  • No tracking. We don't run analytics, advertising scripts, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or social-media trackers on our pages.
  • No data from our tools. The tools run entirely in your browser. Text you type into the Text Editor, Discord Text Formatter or Minecraft Color Codes, and the CPU names you look up in the CPU Compatibility Checker, are processed on your device and are never uploaded to us.

4. Cookies and browser storage

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. Some pages keep small settings in your browser's local storage so the site remembers your choices. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Your chosen theme (Light, Dark or Nighttime) and whether you have dismissed the cookie notice.
  • Tool preferences such as the CPU Checker's settings and recent searches, and the Text Editor's light or dark editor choice.

You can clear this at any time from your browser settings, and the site will keep working.

5. Server logs

Like any website, the servers that deliver our pages may automatically record basic technical details of a request (such as the page requested, the time, and your IP address) to keep the site running and secure. We do not use these logs to track or profile you, and we do not share them with anyone.

6. Links to other sites

Our pages link to other services such as Patreon, Discord, Modrinth and GitHub. If you follow those links you leave our site and those services' own privacy policies apply. We don't pass any of your information to them when you click a link.

7. Children

Our site is general-audience and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Legal requests

Because we don't collect personal information, we have almost nothing to hand over. If we were ever legally required to respond to a valid request, we would only provide what we actually hold, and nothing more.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. We will never change it to allow selling or sharing your data without telling you first.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through our Discord server.
